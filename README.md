SkillCraft Technology- Business Card design concept 

INTRODUCTION

This business card is designed for a premium, craft coffee brand. Unlike a commercial coffee chain, "Artisan Coffee & Roastery" positions itself as a brand focused on quality, authenticity, and the art of roasting. The card serves as a first impression of luxury, warmth, and craftsmanship.

BUSINESS CARD CONCEPT

The core concept is "Modern Luxury meets Artisan Craftsmanship"

1.Front Side (Brand Side):
  The front is purely for brand recall. It features only the logo, brand name, and category. No clutter. The idea is - When you see the card, you remember the brand, not just the person. The dark brown background with gold logo makes it look like a premium product packaging.

2.Back Side (Information Side):
  The back is for connection. It gives clear contact details with a personal touch. The concept is *Minimal & Functional*. The inclusion of:
  - Founder & Head Roaster: Shows it's founder-led and an expert is behind the coffee, building trust.
  - QR Code: Bridges physical card to digital world - Menu / Instagram.
  - Coffee Plant Illustration: Reinforces that the brand is about real beans, origin, and nature.


TAGLINE

Official Tagline: Artisan Coffee & Roastery

This is a descriptive tagline, not a marketing slogan.

- Artisan =Hand-made, small batch, high skill, not mass produced.
- Coffee & Roastery = Tells that they don't just serve coffee, they roast their own beans in-house. This is a big USP in coffee industry.

DESIGN APPROACH

a) Color Palette:
   - Deep Espresso Brown (#2C1A11): Used on front side. Represents coffee beans, warmth, richness, and strength. Creates a premium, bold base.
   - Cream / Beige (#F5F0E8): Used on back side. Represents milk, latte art, paper texture. Gives softness and readability.
   - Metallic Gold (#C9A96E): Used for logo and accents. Represents luxury, premium quality, and warmth. Gold foil effect is intended for print.

b) Typography:
   - Front - "BEAN & BREW": Serif, all caps, wide letter-spacing. Elegant, timeless, and premium - like a luxury fashion brand. Bold and confident.
   - Back - "Aarav Sharma": Bold Serif for name to show authority. "Founder & Head Roaster" in light italic serif - adds a personal, approachable touch.
   - Contact Info: Clean, modern Sans-serif for maximum legibility at small size.
   - Hierarchy: Name > Designation > Contact details.

c) Visual Hierarchy & Layout:
   - Front: Center-aligned, symmetrical layout. Logo on top, Brand Name in middle, Tagline at bottom - balanced and iconic.
   - Back: Left-aligned information, right-aligned graphics. Clear division. Eye naturally reads name first, then designation, then contact icons from top to bottom.

d) Imagery & Iconography:
   - Logo Icon: Minimalist line art of a steaming coffee cup. Simple, memorable, scalable. Steam forms a subtle aroma swirl.
   - Coffee Bean Divider: Small bean icon between two lines shows attention to detail.
   - Botanical Illustration: Detailed line drawing of coffee branch with berries and leaves on back side. Adds an organic, natural, and artisanal feel without being heavy.
   - Contact Icons: Solid brown circular icons (phone, mail, web, location) - modern, uniform, and easy to scan.

e) Printing & Material Approach (Important for Business Cards):
   - Matte Lamination + Gold Foil on front side logo and text.
   - Textured Paper: Suggests kraft or cotton paper to feel natural in hand.
   - Two-Tone Contrast: Dark front and light back makes the card memorable when flipped.
